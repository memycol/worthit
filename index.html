Your data stays in this browser. It is not sent to a server and does not sync across devices. Export a backup if you want to keep your data elsewhere.

Worth It? stores records in browser localStorage. Browser data can be cleared by you, browser policies, or private browsing sessions. It is not encrypted or backed up automatically.

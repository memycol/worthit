# Worth It? — Spend Smarter, Not More

Worth It? is a smart spending calculator designed to help students and young adults make better purchasing decisions. Whether you're considering a new gadget, clothing, subscriptions, or everyday purchases, Worth It? helps you look beyond the price tag and understand what something will actually cost you over time.

Calculate cost per use, compare purchases, set personal spending targets, and reflect on whether an item is genuinely worth buying before spending your money.

**Features include:**
- **Cost-per-use calculator** — estimate what each use of an item will really cost.
- **Purchase comparisons** — compare options to see which offers better value.
- **Spending insights** — understand the long-term cost of your purchases.
- **Personal targets** — set your own value-for-money goals.
- **Quick reflection** — consider whether you need an item before buying it.
- **Saved calculations** — keep track of your decisions.
- **Local data storage** — save your information in your browser.
- **Import and export** — manage your saved data.

No account required. No complicated setup. Just a clearer way to decide whether something is worth your money.
